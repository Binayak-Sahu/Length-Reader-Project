See your head and feet, every shot.

Play a shadow shot. The phone spots it and replays it in slow motion, then goes back to live. Or switch to a delayed mirror.

  1. Prop the phone about 2–3 m in front of you, waist to chest height, where the bowler would be (front-on). Your whole body should be in view.
  2. Tap Start and allow the camera.
  3. Tap the screen any time for controls. Use Set guides once: line on your head in your stance, ring on your head.
  4. Watch for: head staying on the line and inside the ring, front toe to mid-off, and back foot really going back and across.

Pose checks (right-handed batter, camera front-on): head on the line, head still through the shot, and front stride or back-and-across. They need internet the first time to load. Toe angle and base width are left out: one camera can’t judge them reliably.

ADD FROM A CLIP

  1. Find a side-on clip of one delivery (YouTube is fine). Screen-record or save about 5 seconds around it.

Honest limits: one camera only guesses depth, so side-on clips work best; front-on clips make the arm look shaky. Cuts or zooms during the delivery break the tracking. Where the ball goes still comes from the app's physics — lengths and timings stay exact, and the release is pinned to the app's release point.
